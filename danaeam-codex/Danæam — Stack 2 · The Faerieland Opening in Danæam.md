## 2. The text

### I. The 2667 frame
> **Mis Or'ghínhe-îæth'ai yDuý.r.r ythés tack duilín yMedhamýa ghslár-ghüleith Œlimpiad-e atá mith gæn. Uain-éx o slár-cliffýa -anno Domini-. ySíonæiya Ffemá'bissal-iy'dheach-e dús yOthœsræ-othœsræ e Oothraí yiosnÿ yFræli Fœdh-fœdhýa-e o Imadachaíýa-e atá.**

*Literal:* Our 44.27th-year per-i.t., which smackbang within the-middle 10.18th Olympiad-of is we give. One-six and nine-five A.D. Faerieland Appellate-court-of toward-the outlawing of Changing that children the-stealing-of and the-substitution-of is.

*Natural:* It's 2667 I.T., dab in the middle of the 618th Olympiad. 1695 AD. The Faerieland Appellate Court is outlawing 'Changing,' the stealing and substitution of upland children.

### III. The 2997 line
> **Raíwíl-heatth mith gæn. Lus mÿel Fírladr-e yThüfath-aoya lus Sgiesaetthum-e æœá-e niïl Carnfogh — annadh fœrmain œr –'ma — e Oob'aufGaolaí'sígænea sísiyao-e dræœa Raíwíl-ínhe duálthë marcæn.**

*Natural:* It is 2997. The period of 2983 to today marks the Sgiesaetthum's longest without execution, formal or otherwise, of incarcerated Aufgaolaí.

*Natural:* In tiny silver letters spidering the back muscles and abdomen of Red's accuser was the following:

> **Duínráshië afArch úsét Gradhamýa duilín niïl Cruaff e Garrohyê leagístach o guiïn Yon cuirsea o baouea. Dú'iths þis Ó'rrync'ghs o — si'efui'n ythas úsét í scéalerea atá — lus Ghitasiu minú-e yiosnÿ glúmach yAolt Ruth-e dræao atá pliguea.**

*Natural:* In a clutch of yellow elastic tentacles, way up on stage, Night arched and bowed in reverse. Her spine and, written on it, the pull quote from Red's famous article folded toward us.

## 4. Grammar newly attested

1. **Reduplication = the verbal noun.** X-X = "the X-ing": Othœsræ-othœsræ *outlawing*, Insaig-insaig *voting*, Sinótæn-sinótæn *the following*, Farm-farm *the building*, Brís-brís *the breaking*, Fœdh-fœdhýa *the stealing-of*.
2. **-ea, the done participle:** Brísea *broken*, scéalerea *written*, pliguea *folded*, cuirsea *arched*, baouea *bowed* — stacking with stíl- (*stílbrís*, still-breaking).
3. **Ordinals in -eith:** ghslár-ghüleith = the 10.18th (618th). The sealed ordinal; the notes' older *-th* counting forms remain ruled out.
4. **Possessive years:** the Or'…'ai frame ingests a year-name — Or'ghínhe-îæth'ai = "our 2667th-year."
5. **AD digit-mode:** Anno Domini years read as digit-pairs — Uain-éx o slár-cliffýa = 1695 (one-six and nine-five). Distinct from the base-60 year-name mode.
6. **Dates exist by donation:** "Raíwíl-heatth mith gæn" — it is 2997, lit. *we give 49.57*.
7. **Tmesis in derivation and law:** Próitínä'raxis-e'íach (proteinaceous-praxis — the motto ingesting its twin); Oob'aufGaolaí'sígænea (the prison-host ingesting the clade-name); Ffemá'bissal-iy'dheach; Ó'rrync'ghs.
8. **Trill-marks as spatial direction:** ∆ … V marking up/down in running text (the dried-skin passage); ∆barc, the scream goes up.
9. **Names as common nouns:** Red is Ruth, *red (noun)*; Night is Yon, *of the night*; and **Síonæiya is Faerieland** — the realm's endonym, answering where the Síonæyais live.
