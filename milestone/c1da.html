The first restaurant I went to with a robot server had, typed-up, printed, and pasted to the robot’s face, instructions on how to interact. The instructions confused the matter more. Don’t press my eyes. Don’t shout. If something’s missing, wait; I can carry so much. There’ll come a time to query my choices once all’s through. If I disappoint, tell someone else. I never shout. But here I was busy not shouting. Busy thinking “don’t shout*.*” And it couldn’t hear me. So – for the fifth or sixth time in my life – I shouted.
