Dear Amaldéu,

First re: Pasiphaë the Minoan queen, with Cassandra dead Pasiphaë’s my new best sister and friend. She hates to read but likes me to so I do so little but.

Some day —before all the books that are— the queen will be out of things to watch me read. There she’ll be. Pasiphaë. “I want another best friend,” she’ll say.

And me as well. Like— “And I another sister.”

How Paxie delights me! But every book ever’s a lot to read and she won’t be sick of me any sooner, I’m certain. All to say —Amaldéu mestármÿa— my schedule gives but so much slack.

So my Faerish’s gears are salt-stiff and tell me when I mis-steer but you want to know what fucked-through-Hell with this Britt expects of you and, upset and forming a query, you double around, splice ‘spêcidæn’ —‘expected’ or ‘supposed’— inside the gerund for ‘information,’ and ask: “What the fuck am I supposed to do with this information?’? Roughly’s what “Scén ten sís Fiłain- spêcidæn-fiłain-duîm thas Suïfea-duý-Łif duát más spêcidæn?” is?
