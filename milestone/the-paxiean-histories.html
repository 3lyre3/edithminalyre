The Paxiean Histories

Yn te lathermost ancienes of te Ages of Neon, te ancien Pyramyd of Neo-Memphis is a trancen Fenene seacher asclymb.

Tere is te Hi-Far-Inister of Pyramyde sais, yn affisciect of te Tyme, “Who are you? What are you?”

Te trancen Fenene seacher: “I’m Beth. We spoke on the phone. I’m the woman—.”

“Fenene!” is te Hi-Far-Inister asiell (“woman” yn trelt— per te Neoner affisciect). Y morouer Neon: “Beth’s but a name, woman but a word. So too what. So too who. You. The Way Named is not the Eternal. Know this? Again. Who and what are you?”

Y te trancen Fenene: “Sir, I’m here for a job interview."
