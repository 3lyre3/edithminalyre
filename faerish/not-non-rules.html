Not Non-Rules

1 · Orthography & script

  • Letters in use: á â ä ë ê é ï î í ü ú û ł æ œ ô ö ó ý ŷ ÿ; rare þ; V as a letter-mark (the down-trill).
  • Capitalisation: Nouns capitalised, everything else bare. A Noun that becomes a not-noun loses its capital (Mêdhe → mêdhië). Nested capitals drop word-internally; a fused Noun-complex capitalises at its left edge only (afŁamis keeps its mid-word capital as an af-/fel- exception).
  • The seam writes ï: i meeting i across a morph boundary wears the diaeresis (niïl ‹ ni + il; nirïë ‹ niri + -ië).
  • Quotation marks: >– –< top layer, »– –« nested lower layer. English " " appear only inside the imperative's self-quote ("S'aige'in"). faer may tag the close of a quote (>-Łix-< fær Sclíeclu-Sclíeclu sœinea).
  • • (spaced either side) = combined exclamation-mark and colon; opens/closes imperatives, optatives, topic-markers.
  • ∆ = up-trill, V = down-trill. Offices: vocative (up-energy / down-energy; obligatoriness unruled); affect (∆ amused/sharp, V unkind/hushed); spatial ↑/↓ in running text; prose emphasis (V …hushed… beat, ∆ !struck! beat); ∆ also stands for the closing • in the first-person topic-smoosh. One vocable, several offices; typed twin of ∆ is A. Trills run parallel to the segments until sentence-end or the next • — the one exception to prosody-blindness; humans cannot join them. af-/fel- are the trills' dictionary prefixes (up/down).
  • Hyphen = compound junction, toll-free. Apostrophe = tmesis seam, toll due (Ro'math-oo'seoir'b'il). Dash-hung clitic for whole propositions (or'raif'il—'t); seam-dress scales with guest size (presumed law).
  • Imperative punctuation and the vocative trills are written; the fæ majority express them vocally.

2 · Mutations

  • Lenition follows scér, e "of", niïl, and more (Cain→Chain, Bailc→Bailch, Gusci→Ghusci). Eclipsis follows scén. Same split as the two question particles.
  • Pronouns and possessives are mutation-immune (e mao Fía, never *e mhao). Names after e stand unlenited (immunity, or J outside the alphabet — watch).
  • Bare numerals take no mutation.
  • thúil "no longer" is a citation form, not a lenition of *túil.
  • Whether aoa lenites what it negates: unattested — open.

3 · Articles, determiners, definiteness

  • niïl = a/an (ni "fewer-than" + il "multiple"); lus = the.
  • One article slot: lus / niïl / possessives compete for it (*sís lus; mhís e Wíc, never *lus mhís e Wíc). In existentials the possessive bleaches to an article (Mis Scale mith gæn "there is a car").
  • Definiteness is obligatory: proper noun, lus, or enclitic 'ya. Indefiniteness optional.
  • Definite/demonstrative paradigm: Atruens'ya "the incident" (sg, enclitic) · ios Atruens "that" · sís Atruens "this" · sícías Atruensê "these" · iocías Atruensê "those" · Atruensê'ya "the incidents". Demonstratives front the noun and pluralise with cías; the neutral definite rides after the noun, plurality marked on the noun. iocías/sícías are compositional (ios/sís + cías).
  • Proper names are doorless (no article). Whether indefinites make poor topics: unruled.
  • What du can swallow: the article fence alone.

4 · Pronouns & reflexives

  • All bare, never capitalised. ten I · mæ you · fæ 3rd (she/he/they — the universal-fæ system) · í it · mith we · miths us · teo me · tes my · mao your · fær his/her (object form of fæ after iy) · mis our · cías their/them · ír its (an animate/inanimate split beside fær — light fork).
  • Ceremonial force-pronouns outside the fæ-system: Ton/Tonu "he — the creation force" (oblique Tonu-e), þin/þis "she — the noncreation force" (oblique þis-e); declension conditioning unruled.
  • sê, the reflexive: logophoric (in reports, self bends back to the sayer). Naked sê = 1st person "myself"; never 3rd; in infinitives, generic "the-self". Compounds: sê-fær "færself" (fær last — an adjective, taking the in-lieu-of-tmesis position), sê-cías, miths-sê "ourselves", í-sê. Each antecedent pairs with its own compound; no exotic binding conditions. Placement free; also serves as the emphatic. Reflexive placement free.
  • cías-sê: dual duty — alone (sg) / each-other (pl); the alone-construction rides mé, and 'ya on wil reassures. Reciprocity is pronoun-side, never verb-forms; the reciprocal estate is claimed by the plural sê-forms.
  • Clause-mates are disjoint. Otherwise anaphora is direction-free — any direction, domain, distance; no positional sanctuaries at seams; the liberty distributes across o untouched. No special anaphor exists — the pronoun-gender design guards something sneaky, undisclosed by design; the anaphor-ban protects it. One wall anaphora may not pierce: the coordinate island.
  • Picture-Nouns: nothing leaks, no cage — the e-construction available, unrequired.
  • Text-deixis: a six-cell compass, near/far × back/along/plain; the along-column is cataphoric (sísaoya "nearer along", iosaoya "farther along — the yet-to-be-said", sísiya "nearer, direction-unspecified", etc.).
  • Nonspecific office: mith, í, and a conditioned mæ-substitute. someone = uain't-aoa; some = uaothë.
  • 'ya stress / 'thte de-emphasis never change the core pronoun (no obviation). Pronoun emphasis is segmental, both directions. Pronoun-clefts free. Inclusory constructions: yes (mith ssai).
  • Zero subjects licensed where be agrees — a licence, never a law. A dropped copula reads first person.
  • Referentiality and genericness are unmarked.

5 · The verb "to be"

  • One verb, a full grid; two splits govern it: presence of the cleft ios, and number (-s).
  • With ios — and inside y-marked subordinates — the form is obligatorily atá / atáea (pl. atás / atáes). The y-law: "the y in yScéan summons the atá family" — thé (y-less) takes bhie; ythénÿ, yiosnÿ, yScéan take atá. Behind it: existence earns subjecthood; nonexistence is denied it.
  • Elsewhere: bhie(s) present · eibh(s) past · más(as) 1st person · iëao(s) future (iëao = ië- + ao, the morph prefixal, matching its tmetic face 'Ië'…). is: an attested be-face beside bhies (Æœá-e Fía fæ is — gentle fork). aobh: plausibly an optative be-form (held loosely).
  • bhies has a triple life: plural; polite plural; courtesy-singular ("they are a friend") — the last a mystery.
  • Order canon: base OSV; first person SOV ("SO not OS"); colloquial 1st person makes the suis-je — be fronts and fuses: Más-ten (vS-O-V), adjectives hanging free. Ten ærmuch más "I am tired" · Más-ten ærmuch "I'm tired". Más-ten is tonally casual, 1sg only. The overt copula is obligatory by convention but routinely dropped in tmetic fragment-sentences (Stá'fæ'tach "the fæ is stupid").
  • Complement order: 1st person Subject + Complement + be; all others Complement + Subject + be (Oob lus Math lus Seoir bhie "under the mat the key is").
  • Degree-complements: any adjective converts to a degree-word — forcing complement-hood — via Or…il "not X enough" and Ro…il "too X" (Or'lain'il / Ro'lain'il; litotes: or'raif'il—'t "not inadequately hungry" = extremely).
  • Adverbial complements: statives take bare fronted PPs; only action sentences mark direction, with dús (toward-the) and dúïl (toward-a). Formal progressive: Ten dús Fith-Fith más "I toward-the eating am"; semicolloquial Fith-fith: ten fith — first person only (elsewhere it ambiguates).
  • The participle sits before be in 1st person, after be in non-1st (person-conditioned or free — open).
  • Bare subject-complement: the make/crown pattern Mith fær Væx farmea — one dress in both voices.

6 · The existential engine (gæn)

  • Nothing merely is: existence is continuous donation, es gibt taken literally. Frame: í [NP] gæn — "it gives NP" = there is NP; the dummy donor í serves when none volunteers.
  • Possessive existentials: mis "our" / mao "your" as possessives-as-articles, giver mith (Mis Seoir oob mis Math mith gæn).
  • The ours-deferral: when the possessum is genuinely ours in a we-give sentence, one first-person mark defers to second person — the giver flips to mæ, or the article to mao. What is truly ours must be handed to us by you; the community cannot self-certify possession. (In the bleached-article existential, mis certifies nothing, so the deferral is not in play.)
  • Predication by existence: chained gæn-clauses assert identity — … gæn aich sin … gæn "is also a".
  • The gift test: an NP is what gæn can give — the engine doubles as the constituency-detector (tmesis words travel as Nouns). Second jurisdiction: resumability as the topic-licence — a topic is what í may say.
  • Tenure: plain gæn undefined and common; gænyin temporally bound tenure; gilæn eternal regiving (g'[w]il'æn — wil "all" tmesized into gæn, the hard w paid, welded solid). mith gæn = inalienable; í gæn = alienated. gæn wears tense — the six-form chant; -ea past and ië- future productive across the family (gænea, ïegæn't).
  • The naming-split: the unnamed is possessed from the front; the named is tagged from behind (Kettlina-mine); ours and theirs by the same pattern.
  • Negative existential: æœá Frælithi'ya í gænyin — nonexistence denied subjecthood, the dummy í giving.
  • Dates exist by donation: Raíwíl-heatth mith gæn "it is 2997".
  • States by frame: gæn mé X Craimh "X is hungry" (es-gibt × tá-ocras-orm).
  • gæn vs mé-e (donation-ownership vs accompaniment-having): division presumed, unruled; tes-mé possession-as-accompaniment enriches the mé side.

7 · Negation, polarity & comparison

  • Answer words: aobha "no" (ao "not" + bha "is"), bháya "yes" (bha + 'ya), Syá "maybe" ('thte + 'ya ground together by fast speech). The verb-echo + clitic answer is commoner.
  • Polarity clitics: 'ya positive · 't negative post-consonant (Fith't, thurn't) · 'thte negative post-vowel (Nhœme'thte, scé'thte) · 'ma on-the-contrary (Gæn'ma "oh but I am"). The weld mÿa ‹ 'ma + 'ya.
  • Leading tags: scé'ya (expecting yes), scé'thte (expecting no). Under ellipsis a clitic can outlive its host (Œr 'thte? — scé elides, 'thte hangs).
  • The clitics dock on the focused constituent of any class (duíra'ya, Eg'ya / Eg't). Bare aoa = constituent negation; 't = the polar stamp; film't alone is grammatical but "disagreeably subtle".
  • Double negative = attenuation, not cancellation (aoa duíxeis film't ≈ "semi-rightward-turn-ness"); double negatives are disembodiers. Neg-raising is the default; low docking disambiguates, aoa-fronting wilfully re-ambiguates.
  • Negation fusion runs the whole tense system: atáea'thte "wasn't" · gilæn't "never-gave" (doubled gilæn't gilæn't "never, never") · ïegæn't "will-not-give". The general negative strategy, not a present-tense quirk.
  • The pro-verb át "did" answers for any verb, first-person order-flip intact: Ten ios át "I did" · Ios mæ át "you did".
  • æœá = the object-no, direct on the NP; æœá-e "without / of-no" (Ten æœá-e Fía más). æœá-e leads its NP (determiner-blood); mé-e trails (preposition-blood). The from/without twins: dræao and æœá-e.
  • Negative-quantity scale (on partitive e + 'ya): niuain · aoa uain · æouain · ssíns.
  • Comparison is built from the polarity clitics: X'ya = -er degree (slee'ya "faster" — reads as the 'ya-degree; the true comparative suffix is -mÿa, sleemÿa; gentle fork), 'ya'ma even-more. The standard takes iy "than": Holdhmÿa iy tens "taller than me". iy never omits — coherence keeps its anchor. Attributively the head Noun tmesizes into its standard, -iy hanging by the tmeter: Niïl holdhmÿa Fæ'hín-iy'ær "a man taller than fær". The repeated adjective must vanish, its copula may stay: iy lus Sídia ___ (atá). iy + NP is a bare phrase — no silent structure; clausehood exists exactly where y-marking shows it. Comparative clauses shed everything but the standard-NP.
  • -mÿa generalises the comparative to adverbs; contrariety wears -ma. Superlatives: mÿel "more-than-any" / niyel "fewer-than-any" (with variant definite articles; partitive e placements). Equative: the sin…sy correlative — sin mordach sy fær "as big as her" (in practice sy fær sin mordach fæ bhie); reaches noun-function ("as a club") and adverbs. "very" = the duíra'ya boost (borderline, awaiting word).
  • The emphasis kit is asymmetric: yes doubles (bháya'ya, fith'ya bha); no chains its lone morpheme (fith't o 't o 't). Combinations free; the chain grades open-endedly, whimsy over scarcity.
  • Conjunct-glow: X'ya o Y "X indeed, and Y" — conjunct-emphasis is a polarity act by the docking law; one conjunct may glow alone; many-glows = two 'ya stamps. Negative glows dress as 'tya / 'thteya, avoiding added negators (split-pole X'ya o Y'tya; bare 't stays plain negation). An 'ma-stamp ('maya): unattested.

8 · The interrogative system

  • A clefting, particle-driven, prosody-blind, movement-only system. All questions are obligatorily tagged — no tonal questionhood exists.
  • Particles: scér (past, leniting) / scén (non-past, eclipsing), placed at a notably irregular point. Suspended protases ask honestly: scér tmeses into ann (O scé'ann mao Myth dús bhie Son-Son e bhie? "and if your nose is a-bleeding?").
  • The retro-repeater (r.r.): the sentence's last verb repeated in advance at a notably nonregular point — the audible signifier of questionhood beyond the particle. Emphasis/urgency ∝ irregularity of placement; at the extreme the copy erupts mid-word (to- are -ward). R.r.s are questions-only; declaratively their mirror is plain repetition (§12). The r.r. alone is position-free.
  • The frozen cleft ios: resonance-governed, never question-bound, not obligatory in questions; obligatory wherever thas stands (ios attends thas, never the reverse — mystery). Dropped in echoes, where even thas surrenders it: the missing cleft is the tell. Demonstrative-ios ≠ cleft-ios (rides plain bhie, no y-marking).
  • Multiple-wh fronting: nothing sits in situ; every questioned element fronts, stacked. The th-word's home is clause-initial after the particle; a subject ahead of the wh is an error — except a first-person subject, which flips the order.
  • thurn is inherently interrogative — no non-question form. here = sís thurn; there = ios thurn (existence stated with gæn: Í ios thurn niïl Nímhog gæn). them "how" (frame them…yail "how many"); thénu whom-oblique (after prepositions; whose = thénu mé); ythemnÿ "how" in reported speech.
  • Echo-questions (in-situ and intonation both banned) are solved in the morphology: echo prefix gla- (reduced glés- for one-word echoes) + vowel-harmonising suffix -as: Glascér … laías? / Glésthas? "WHAT?". Informal echo: the verb reduplicates and burrows inside the wh-word (Sclaí-laí-laí-laí-tha-laí-s mæ?). Statement-echo uses gla-…-as; question-echo re-poses the clause + tag (…scén scé'ya) with deixis shifted (mæ→ten). No ios in echoes — the echo absorbs it: cleft = ordinary question, no cleft = echo.
  • Answers, yes–no: echoed verb + clitic (Fith'ya · Fith't / Nhœme'thte · Gæn'ma) or dedicated bháya / aobha / Syá; the pro-verb át for any verb. Echo-answers drift toward 'ya (Bháya. Ghusci'ya. — the Danæam "you heard me").
  • Wh-answers are the question's negative space: the focus fronts to exactly the th-word's slot; the cleft goes implicit; no particle, no r.r. In subclauses the implicit cleft holds the ghost y-…nÿ, so every overt th-containing clause takes bare y-. Fragments may fuse into one word by tmesis (C'achér'ed "second drawer").
  • Long-distance questions mark the path only where it matters: y-…nÿ marks the th-element in the first subordinate clause that itself contains one (yiosnÿ, yésnÿ); bare y- marks th-elements in all secondary clauses (yüs, yés). Binary — no third grade; agreement tracks th-content, not the abstract path. A relative clause with no internal th-element stays plain ios. "Th-element" = membership in the interrogative paradigm (thas…thés); surface th- alone does not qualify (thúil is not one); sís thurn does put one inside its clause.
  • Rootness: • and scér/scén are root-clause-makers; y-…nÿ / y- fire in true subordinates only.

9 · The imperative

  • Danæam does not command; it announces compliance and directs the listener's attention to their own obedience, already underway. Frame: Air • "S'aige'in" dúirá film. — [Notice • "so-said" leftward turn] = "Turn left." Air "notice!" opens; • presents; "S'aige'in" (sin wrapped around s-shorn saige) is the self-quotation. No particle, no r.r., no ios: its own sentence-type.
  • Fragments drop the so-said nest and reduce the particle (Air → Ai', as gla- → glés-). Persons nest into the command, shedding their onset (Ai'æ'lith); Air's r survives as liaison only before i- and e-initial nested pronouns. Bare Ai'[verb] is implicitly 2pl unless context narrows it. fæ cannot nest in imperatives (official answer: Syá).
  • Two axes of degree: urgency scales by compression (bare Ai'lith! maximal); gravity/anger by ceremonious expansion — syntactically proper embeddings of air plus expecting-yes questionoids (Sís mæ-film air, scén scé'ya? • "S'aige'in" dúirá film.). The more formally complete, the more trouble you are in. The prohibitive does not invert the scaling.
  • Polarity clitics attach for force: 'ya do-emphasis; 't / 'thte prohibition (Ai'lith't! "don't run" — the announcement simply announces non-compliance). 1pl prohibitive: Air'ith'rubh't "we touchn't"; audibility variant dash-set Air'ith'rubh —'thte—!; periphrastic Sís mith air —'thte— • "S'aige'in" í aoa rubh. 1pl hortative: Ai'ith'rubh'ya "let's touch!" — 't forbids, 'ya invites.
  • The optative: O • frames it (superseding Ai); Mee converts optative → imperative, carrying the implicit existential [í] [subject] Mee [gæn] — curses are gifts (O • Mee aoa mhís e Wíc linchs). Mee is monitory; its geas-tiers Me'æ'e (2sg), Me'en'e (1sg). The elliptical optative also underlies the topic-shifter ("May what follows concern X"); its reach beyond that frame unruled.
  • No debitive exists — the imperative serves.

10 · The tmesis engine

  • One mechanism, five coats: things that go inside other things pay a toll and leave a seam. The two laws: the apostrophe marks every seam; the nested word sheds its onset consonant.
  • The toll's phonology: hard onsets pay (drop): b c d g j k p q t w x. Soft onsets ride free. In clusters, hard members drop and soft survive (Thrugh → 'rugh; Wíc → 'íc; Malach keeps its m). Pronouns pay regardless of softness (mæ → 'æ, mith → 'ith).
  • Seam-merge: identical sibilants across a seam merge (S'aige'in ‹ Sin + saige). Sin = "so/as", so S'aige'in is "as-said" — the verbs-into-Sin domain is the as-X-ed adverbial factory. Sin's split-shapes: S'—'in · S'—'n · si'—'n · s'aige'n — the 'in → 'n elision freer than suspected, perhaps free variation.
  • The five coats: the r.r. burrows into wh-words (informal echo); the r.r. into ordinary words (extreme emphasis); Nouns nest inside adjectives (C'achér'ed = ced split around (t)achér; endless adjective-nesting allowed, adverbs never nest); verbs nest inside Sin; pronouns nest inside the imperative (Ai'æ'lith — to command someone, Danæam ingests them).
  • Host-minimality & the tmeter: a guest whose host is too small to open hyphen-dangles instead (mé-saige — its saige a plain r.r.; -iy inside Fæ'hín-iy'ær). Working hypothesis: the host must leave a viable shell on both sides of the seam (mé's right shell would be a bare é).
  • Welds (fusion proper): true fusion writes solid — Syá, ythús, iostá — no seam-mark, toll still paid (ythús ‹ yth + (l)us). Law: "they fuse where the energetic resonance serves (it's vibe based)" — optional always, obligatory never. The weld is also the complex-preposition formation (wythil · duílín · drúræao · dúiri).
  • Stacking & order: proximity decided by relevance first, English-style rank as tie-breaker; tmesized, the stack goes concentric — leftmost = outermost shell (re'bi'o'car'ld'g'd). Tmeting is optional — always.
  • The wrapper carries the grammar, the core carries the meaning; a core may itself be reduplicated (Dú'greibach-greibach-e's). Guests may be pure cry (Ai'æ'titlop -luai- "note-you-topple, aiee!"). Monster tmeses attested: ro'gænstílgæn'il · Dé'ahor'cruí'sáillaí'ch'ía'er · aufGao'samsca'laíê · Gi'regéar'gýa · stil'ten'át "sti'I'll-do". Names and whole clade-names ingest (Oob'aufGaolaí'sígænea "incar'aufGaolaí'cerated"; Or'ghínhe-îæth'ai ingests a year-name); derivation can ingest its own twin (Próitínä'raxis-e'íach).
  • Whole clefts are emphasised by death-and-resummoning as deep tmesis.

11 · Coordination & ellipsis

  • Coordinators: o "and" (between every pair, or before the last) · œr "or" · ax "but; but-especially" (n−1 pattern; extended to selective emphasis: Mith ax ten'ya) · æxó (NAND-flavoured) · aoæx "either-or-neither" (disjunction with a refusal built in; patterns as æxó) · aoœr "neither-nor". Coordination is category-blind and function-parallel — form irrelevant. O the list-opener ("all" modifier) opens a coordination as a menu — options offered, both exist, soft both-at-once; only with o / œr / ax, never æxó/aoæx; distinct from the optative O, which wears the •.
  • Reach-back coordination: no verb-category is barred from coordinating; the only restriction is the limits of the preceding phrase. Each o-conjunct bonds back to whatever layer of the opener coheres — and may return to an earlier layer after a deeper one. Hearers attach clauses back as their judgment serves.
  • Omission under identity: the second conjunct may drop a repeated be.
  • Phrase-level ellipsis (inside NP / AdjP / AdvP alike): gap or uain, as the resonance serves — the bare gap rides the hearer's reach; uain is the stand-in body.
  • Fragments are unrestricted — only permissions, no restrictions; orphaned clitics permitted (Œr 'thte); a solitary owned fragment is allowed and nongrammatical (Holdhmÿa.), but iy never stands over a ghost (*Holdhmÿa iy.).

12 · Emphasis

  • The deafness law: loudness carries no meaning; no meaning rides melody anywhere (no tone exists; intonation subtypes imply nothing, ever). Segments alone carry meaning.
  • No emphasis by movement — order-freedom is answerhood's alone. Emphasis rides repetitions and clitics.
  • The triarchy: cleft for constituents · the kit (polarity clitics) for polarity · plain repetition for verbs — the r.r.'s declarative mirror (Fær mæ łathn! Łathn!).
  • Declarative emphasis-clefting is granted; sentence-opening clefters stand bare. Pseudo-clefting in two grades: Sin uain ythénÿ… and the bare-opening Thé… (bare = no y…nÿ).
  • The decleft doctrine: the atá-family is the sentence's cleft-memory; iostá is the manual de-clefter; emphasis docks free within frames.
  • NP-internal emphasis: the resumptive-í frame — NP first, í reflecting, the general be closing; no ios, so never atá (ending on atá is proscribed); the whole NP pied-pipes, the part never travels alone. The resumptive í is not the reflexive í.
  • Dislocation is refused as an emphasis device — no hanging themes left or right. The two-tier system: movement proper strands nothing (the semicolloquial Fith-fith: ten fith excepted, 1st person only); dislocation resumes with í (things) / fæ (persons); no particles.
  • Emphatic-pronoun cells: standing machinery (segmental, both directions).

13 · Topic

  • Three marker devices: S'air'n · O • o e X • · Efui • — plus the for-its-part replica s'efui-e'in (placed after its topic). Sin X is the plainest road, running bare. Boundary-marks are conditioned per device (colon · closing • · bare). The shifter's logic: an elliptical optative, "May what follows concern X".
  • Markers stack, unranked; a marker may take the for-its-part replica as reprise. The thinnest marker suffices, and the resumptive elides beneath it (whether an overt í/fæ may also stand: open).
  • No topic by bare movement — never without a marker. The first-person smoosh: O X ∆, the trill standing for the closing • (O Scóhn ∆ ten mac).
  • Position is whatever: marked topics ride parenthetically anywhere an auxiliary fits. Initial = the first seam after the particles. Other positions live in the seams between clauses, never phrase-internally. The final seat stays closed — the be owns the door; afterthoughts only as new pointing clauses on the Œr 'thte model; the tail is a topic-reminder riding the post-clausal seam.
  • Contrastive topics: routed through the tmesis-fold road — swallow, then stamp the tail ('ya).
  • The marker split: bare for glow (emphasis), marked for topic.
  • Topic-licence = resumability, the gift test's second jurisdiction: a topic is what í may say. Adjectives and adverbials cannot topicalise as themselves; verbs enter by the gerund door.
  • The topic-path law: free at any depth; first subordinate plain, second-and-deeper bare y- (the nÿ never worn). The y-system's second office. (Two y-offices crossing in one sentence: precedence unruled.)
  • The coordinate island holds: no travel, no in-situ dress, every marker moonlighting.
  • The topic stack: free multiplicity; every secondary topic an object; predicates pair first-to-first by verb-last discipline; a referent may return; no one-topic law. No topic agreement, ever.

14 · NP syntax & the function-country

  • Genitive/of: e serves both bearings — Hín e Gree "a man of humour" fronts the quality; lus Gree e lus Hín "the humour of the man" reverses it; order alone disambiguates. e's of-office spans quality, quantity, reference, partitive (general to quantifiers) — not material, not cause.
  • Quality also derives directly: -ach (Stát→Státach; Grëach Hín) — a parallel road. Negative quality inherits the circumstance apparatus: aoa atá, atá'thte, or thé + æœá-e + bhie — the quality denied subjecthood.
  • Quantity: one frame, Q e N — Niïl Scál e thíthíl Tonnaê "a boat of a thousand tons"; Niïl Lund e Shar "a pound of sugar"; the numeral absorbs one layer of of.
  • Material: by, not e — Tïc by Úrsapê "a house of bricks"; means and matter merge. Compare Tïc e Úrsapfarmaí (abstract/industry association) and Tïc dúe Úrsap-Farm-farm (purposive: a house for the trade).
  • Manner: three co-valid roads — by-instrumental (by Elin ghuethnea "with verve served"); tmesis (El'huethnea'in "ve'served'rve"); -ië adverb (elinië ghuethnea). Negative: honestly open.
  • Cause: a family of four metaphors, and by never enters — origin e (Ærmuchaí e Síulnê "exhaustion of wounds"); gift-given gæn (fær Síulnê fær Ærmuchaí gænea); gift-received dræao + ghiæan (fæ Ærmuchaí fær Síulnê dræao ghiæanea); destination duín + tug (Yth fær Síulnê fæ duín Ærmuchaí tugea eibh "brought to exhaustion by"). yth may front a cause over a plain passive (Yth ír Ralxú lus Both rüstea eibh — whether a true passive-agent device or adverbial fronting is open).
  • Function ("used as"): the sin…sy equative stretched to nouns — Ten sy Cthug sin lus Acusthia lynœa "I as Club so the stick used"; the sy-phrase fronts to the object slot.
  • Reference ("about"): e — Ten fær e Atruens'ya saíea "I him of the incident told".
  • Essive ("as a soldier"): the possessed mé — tes-mé Maghr "my-with soldier-being"; accompaniment made possessive, a state carried. Also attested mao-mé. Functive frame: duín X tes-mé Yr (duín Berolth tes-mé Maghr).
  • Translative ("appointed as"): same structure as become — the postposed hyphen-joined fær-dúe flag + -r state-noun object + causative verb (Mith fær-dúe Xæxr ghiæanea "made him king"; Mith fær-dúe Cáithoorhr oothíthea "appointed him general"). A goal-dress division glimpsed: -r state-nouns travel with ghiæan/oothíth; farm takes bare Væx; the du-dress holds Duílúch.
  • Partitive: e presides; the enclitic 'ya is the default definiteness inside it (ssai e Frælithi'ya "two of the children"); lus does not appear there. Partitive quantifiers use the same Q-e frame.
  • Benefactive vs recipient: doing-for wears dúe (Ten dúe Piell í átea); giving-to keeps duîm (Ten duîm Piell í gæn) — benefaction never swallowed by gift. dúe's three offices: purposive linker (dúe fith), infinitive-subject flag (dúe sê), benefactive; also "for / out of" (Byræao Stár dúe Ruth "out of love for Red"). dúe's two seats (preposed dúe sê; postposed fær-dúe): conditioning unruled; dúe/dué accent fork open.
  • Price rides dúe. Value: the e-frame, with the Lyr/Lund currency-weight split and the worth-relative. Distance and duration: bare measure-NPs. Extent: the N-e-measure frame. "ago" = duáo + measure.
  • Circumstance ("with X"): positive — the relative packs it, mé-e trailing, atá (the accompaniment stands as temporary second subject): Niïl Hín ythénÿ reinach Sáinsu mé-e atá. Negative, three roads: aoa atá · atá'thte · restructured with thé + æœá-e + plain bhie — the absent thing denied subjecthood; the be-choice tracks existence.
  • Prepositions & relational words: mé with · e of · by via/through/as-process · dræao from (second office: the passive agent — the will; by the channel) · yth past/over/beyond · Oob under · In at/in (time: category-blind) · duín in/into (state arrived at) · dús toward-the / dúïl toward-a · u- = du- toward (the du→u reform declared — "horrible confusion" expected; check every u-/du- sighting) · duáf upward (variant dúaf) · ufel · dúy to-through · uÿth · aoíth before (bare time-Nouns presumed) · aoyth like (recorded a preposition; near-pair with aoíth) · wythil despite · metałie inclusive-of · duílín within · drúræao since/after (the posterior country whole) · dúiri until/unto (also the honorific) · duáo ago. P+P compounds exist (duín ‹ du + In). Exclusion = the negated y-clause; addition = the aich-chain.
  • Attributive adjectives: two strategies side by side — Adj-lead (reinach Sáinsu, Iarnach Ixixaí) and tmesis-fold; pronoun-compounds put the adjective last (sê-fær). A presumed blood-law: æœá-e leads its NP; mé-e trails.
  • Vocative: the trills ∆ / V; intimacy Scéan-teos. The familiar possessive [kin]-[absolute] (rhié-færs, sídia-teos) — kin-noun lowercase; reaches epithets as mock-honorific (státachaí-færs "His Idiocy"); presupposes the known bond. The te…'os kin-frame (te'rhié'os "parent-mine") reads as te-(person) + 'os possessive-former (unconfirmed).
  • The absolute construction: usually a topic clause wearing an epithet — O X ∆ / O X V (O státachaí-færs ∆ dúe sê fæ niïl Pat sulænea).
  • The infinitive: the toward-prefix on the verb — u- makes usót "to go"; subjects ride dúe (dúe sê … usót); goals prefix likewise (ułiuafth "Heavensward"). Preferred reading: the topic clause fronts as an as-frame (S'łiuafth-e'in — dúe sê S'eg'in usót).
  • No heavy-shift, ever: weight goes to a fresh Aich—sentence or packs into the ythénÿ-relative; verb-last absorbs it.
  • Nonrestrictive relatives ride clefts: the paradigm ythé/ythénÿ // ythés/ythésnÿ.
  • Labels: the fragment-licence's own case. Honorific style: Alcuilaí, dúiri. The self-vow reads as intention. Purpose: by dúe and by by.
  • Incorporation: subjects never; IOs may.
  • We-firemen: apposition with mé (mith Łúgrchainr-mé "we firemanbeing-with").

15 · Voice, aspect, mood

  • Passive: participle + eibh, patient as subject (farmea eibh); tense-transparent. The agent is where the deed comes from: a dræao-source (Dræao lus Ufín Ooraithr fæ ghiæanea — killed-being received as a dark gift; existence-as-donation speaking even in death). Other-constituent passives yes; IO passives no. Object-drop is free (which verbs resist: watched). Impersonals form from three verb-types.
  • Causative: ghiæan by frame (also the become-copula: Duílúch Fæ'du'ærs fæ ghiæan; and "get over": by ghiæan). Becoming/appointing: fær-dúe + -r state-noun + causative verb (ghiæan, oothíth). Ditransitive causatives yes; the causee omissible. Dynamic vs static = ghiæan vs eibh. Anticausative: silence.
  • The becoming-gift is also the passive-with-agent's frame; ghiæan takes du-marked goal-states (Duílúch).
  • Aspect: bare = perfective · dús [X-X] = progressive/imperfective (whether it wraps statives like "knowing": watched) · gnomic = the habitual · ghiæan = ingressive · tenure = iterative · stíl- = the perfect, in all three tenses (Ten stílsótea; double pluperfect Ten úteo tes St'sót-sót'íl stílgænea; future perfect Iëao As í iëátea) · thuíl = the terminative, four free orders · the one-form perfect keeps its several readings. stil "still" (phase) pairs with thúil "no longer"; stíl- stacks on participles (stílbrís "still-breaking").
  • Mood: Syá = potential and contingent; the certainty ladder is assembled from standing pieces; Mee = monitory; aich sin = consecutive.
  • The Verbless Eternal Past and Future (world / worldn't — the ceremonial deeps): Gil (not Gil't) with its own semantics; Hthom swallows propositions whole; the =-chain links (name and reach open); the coda þis-e stands (typed bis-e ratified as the keyboard's toll); force-pronouns Ton and þin.
  • Tenure-predication ratified. 2983-class truths mark by donation and marcæn.

16 · Derivation & morphology

  • -ea = the done participle / past (Brísea "broken", scéalerea "written", pliguea "folded", gænea "given", farmea "made", átea, atáea, craigea, laíea, sulænea, rüstea…). ië- = future. -'thte/-'t fuse for the negatives across all tenses.
  • Reduplication X-X = the verbal noun / gerund ("the X-ing"): Fith-Fith, Farm-farm, Brís-brís, Othœsræ-othœsræ "outlawing", Insaig-insaig "voting", Sinótæn-sinótæn "the following", greibach-greibach "the believing", Fœdh-fœdhýa, Glaoh-Glaoh, Côs-Côs, Chís-Chís, shrai-shrai, Crith-crith, Sclíeclu-Sclíeclu, Drúnhœme-Drúnhœme, Útcár-Útcár. Reduplication is the gerund-maker; the doubled stem names the act itself, and articles/genitives wrap it as any noun. Reflexives survive nominalisation by the eës-split gerund (Eë'shrai-shrai's sê-fær); no possessive-subject scaffold. Nouns from clauses: the fold-road plus the gift test. Fith-Fith (formal gerund) vs Fith-fith (colloquial, lowered second capital): split presumed deliberate.
  • -ach = quality adjective (Stát→Státach, Grëach, reinach). -aí = the abstract former (Státachaí "idiocy", Grëachaí, Ærmuchaí, mordachaí, Alcuilaí; skips -ach when -ach would be absurd: Úrsapfarmaí "brickmakery"). -ië = noun/degree-word → adverb (mêdhië, nirïë, elinië, mordachië, craithië; also Ghraíïe "fourily"). -r = the state/unit-noun former "-being" (Xæxr, Maghr, Statr, Gheor, Cáithoorhr, Ooraithr, wínr as suffixoid, and the unit-road for masses: uain Wícr). -æn = the verbalizer (elf/elfæn; yiusétæn). -iya = one-who / that-which, agentive-relativising (Thérafthyiya "therapist", Líciya, Sísiyaoýa). -os = possessive-former in the kin-frame (te'rhié'os). -eith = the ordinal (ainteith "first", ghslár-ghüleith). -ê = the pluraliser, plain and simple — not numeral-exclusive (Úrsapê, Síulnê, Tonnaê, Nímhogê, Frælithi irregular); one plural, with phrasal distributivity. -as = the echo suffix, vowel-harmonising — the attested case of suffix-harmony. af-/fel- = orientational-affect prefixes (up 🔥 / down; the Noun's capital survives mid-word: afŁamis, felCne…, afStrœ…).
  • Adjectives derive from adjectives via the degree circumfixes (Or…il / Ro…il) and -mÿa. Adverbs DO modify adverbs — through the degree/attenuator syntax, not a dedicated suffix (Ten í or'mordachië'il—'t craithië fithea "not-insufficiently-enormously hungrily ate"). The attenuator family roïl/oril/or…il/ro…il: headcount watched.
  • Tmesis-fold words are Nouns by the gift test. Derivation is closed: no further derivational machinery beyond the above.

17 · Numerals & dating

  • Year-names, base 2+30 (base 60): a year-name has two places, A-B = A×60 + B, hyphen-joined; the first element capitalises, the second goes bare. Each place (1–60) is one of 30 pair-stems × 2 forms: ODD wears the bare stem, EVEN its gh- twin — mutation doing numerical duty. Attested stems: Slár 9 / Ghslár 10 · Ül 17 / Ghül 18 · Sfeith 21 / Ghsfeith 20 · Îæth 27 / Ghîæth 28 · Ínhe 43 / Ghínhe 44 · Raíwil 49 / Ghraíwil 50 · Heatth 57 / Gheatth 58. OPEN: the Sfeith pair runs {20, 21} against the others' {odd, odd+1} — direction unruled; unseen stems await.
  • Canon checks: 44.27 Ghínhe-îæth = 2667 I.T. (Changing outlawed) · 49.21 Raíwíl-sfeith = 2961 (the Oath's end) · 49.43 Raíwíl-ínhe = 2983 (the Bródlainn riots) · 49.57 Raíwíl-heatth = 2997 (the present) · 10.18 Ghslár-ghül = 618 (the Olympiad). One Sgiesaetthum = 120 years = 2.0.
  • The analytic counting forms (raí-ghíl-ssaighisseith and kin) are not used; the year series is the one numeral canon.
  • Ordinal: -eith (ghslár-ghüleith Œlimpiad-e "the 10.18th Olympiad"). Possessive year: the Or'…'ai tmesis frame (Mis Or'ghínhe-îæth'ai yDuý.r.r "our 2667th-year"). AD years read as digit-pairs, not base-60 (Uain-éx o slár-cliffýa -anno Domini- = 1695); digit-mode stems: Uain 1, Cliff 5, Éx 6, Slár 9 (their year-series pairs unruled). Dates exist by donation (Raíwíl-heatth mith gæn "it is 2997"); spans mark with marcæn.
  • Small counting cardinals (not year-stems; the converter's stem table unaffected): 1 Aint · 2 Ghaint · 3 Raí · 4 Ghraí — unmarked odd, gh- even, the year-stems' own pairing; ordinal ainteith "first"; 5 and 6 unmade. Counting table so far: 0 ssíns · 2 ssai · 5 cliff · 6 éx · 8 As · 1000 thíthíl.
  • After numerals the noun stands in the counted -ê form (éx Nímhogê, thíthíl Tonnaê); the numeral+counted-ê phrase fills e's complement in measure frames. Bare numerals take no mutation. Attributive numerals and the compound-quantifier road: settled standing machinery.

18 · Phonology & prosody

  • Prosody-blind: no fixed word-accent; no canonical peak; no length; no tone; no segmental effects from intonation. The trills ∆/V are the one exception, running parallel to the segments.
  • Local suffix-harmony (-as attested). Vowels free under the seam-law. No consonant harmony; no phonotactic restrictions beyond seam-and-toll. þ and V are loan-flavoured.

19 · The mysterious ios

  • ios is bound to thas — obligatory wherever thas stands; "ios is the eternal companion of thas, not thas of ios". Elsewhere ios arrives as the resonance demands. Echoes strip it even from thas.
